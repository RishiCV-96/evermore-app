@AGENTS.md

## Evermore framework (applies to every change)

Every change must serve this framework. `DESIGN.md` records the verdicts and what is built. Update it when a verdict changes.

**Core function:** Help a person with early memory loss keep recognizing the important people in their life, through short, personal fill-in-the-blank stories.

**Core loop (under 30 seconds):**
- Trigger: a card appears with a large photo and one sentence with a blank, e.g. "My mom, ______, and I went to Santa's Village every December."
- Action: the person taps one of 2 to 3 name choices.
- Feedback: a wrong choice shows a gentle hint and lets them try again. Every wrong tap, the same name again included, moves the hint exactly one step (since 2026-11-16, `docs/HINT-LADDER.md`): never two for one tap, never back, never past the answer; a name once chosen is dimmed and disabled, and once the hint says the answer only that name is live. Never use red, "wrong," or scores. Every card ends on a success. **I'm not sure** (built 2026-11-13), a quiet control under the names, starts the same hints with no name chosen (the hard rule below).
- Reward: the full sentence is read back with the photo and a warm confirmation.
- Variety (built): the same fact can also be asked as a question ("Who loves the Riverton Otters?"), as "Who is this?" with a fact, or as a photo-only card ("Who is this?"). Every card shows the photo, and the caregiver can turn any phrasing off. The one exception is the reveal card (see the hard rules). With AI on, approved AI sentences join these, including the reverse card, which gives the name and asks for a fact.

**Screen budget:** 8, all used:
1. Sign-in (the welcome, the person's sign-in with Their login, and the caregiver's forms, as steps)
2. Person home
3. Practice card
4. Caregiver dashboard (Today, Trends, People and Settings tabs)
5. Add a person
6. Story preview and approval
7. Session wrap-up
8. Insights (the caregiver's fifth tab, since 2026-11-10)

Add modals (`Sheet`) or sections, never new screens. Insights became its own tab on 2026-11-10, a deliberate change to the budget (it was a view under Trends); the person-facing flow is unchanged.

**Not built yet (don't start without being asked):**
- the retention hook (unfinished story)
- push notifications from a server (the person's daily reminder, since 2026-11-13, is a local notification the phone schedules itself; see the hard rules)
- payments
- more than one person per caregiver
- more than one caregiver per family
- AI sentences with sign-in on: they work with sign-in off only (`AI_AVAILABLE` in `src/config/features.ts`), because approved sentences live on the phone. Sign-in is on by default, so they show only in the local-only mode

**Hard rules:**
- Story text comes from the caregiver's words placed into fixed templates, with one bounded exception: AI may generate NEW sentences from the caregiver's approved facts, so the app tests real recall instead of pattern recognition. It may rephrase and recombine them, and may never add, infer, soften or change a fact. Every one of these bounds holds:
  - **Off by default.** `AI_VARIANTS_ENABLED=false` in `src/config/features.ts`. With it off, nothing about AI shows and nothing is sent. Turned on, it still needs the caregiver's one-time consent, and can be turned off in Settings at any time, or for one person on their sheet. It needs no sign-in and must never add a login wall.
  - **Facts only.** One person's approved facts per request, sent only when the caregiver taps Generate.
  - **No photos sent.** Only text ever leaves the phone for AI.
  - **The name is never sent.** The person's name becomes `[NAME]`, every other name or proper noun becomes `[PERSON_1]` and so on, and card ids become `f1`. Only the phone can turn them back.
  - **Never time-sensitive or test_data facts.** Time-sensitive facts are never reworded: they appear only in the fixed, dated templates. Your real people from the local deck are never sent either (see "No invented facts").
  - **Validated in two places.** The function checks every sentence before returning it, and the phone repeats the checks after restoring the real words, so a bad response can't slip through. Both run `src/lib/ai-check.ts`. A rejected sentence is dropped silently. Every word must come from the cited facts, their kind's fixed words, the relationship or a short list of small words, and every content word of a cited fact must stay, so nothing can be softened.
  - **Caregiver approval.** Every sentence waits in the queue on the person's sheet, with its source facts, for approve, edit or reject. An edit is checked again. Nothing reaches the person until approved, and a sentence whose fact changes or goes leaves practice at once and is flagged for review.
- Answers can't be learned by pattern: the other names offered and the position of the right name rotate, so the same person never gets the same names in the same order twice in a row.
- Only cards the caregiver approved reach practice. New and edited memories wait for approval; skipped ones never appear. RLS enforces this for the person too. **Approve all** on a person's sheet (`src/lib/approve-all.ts`) approves only what one tap each would, and never a fact that may change, an edited or flagged AI sentence, or one that fails the checks; it is offered only once that person's waiting list has been opened in the session, confirms first, and has Undo. On story approval (screen 6), where every story is on screen, **Approve all** (`storyApproval`) approves the waiting stories the same rule allows in one tap, with Undo and no confirmation: never a fact that may change, a story open for editing, or an AI sentence (that screen doesn't show them), and skipped stories stay skipped.
- **The photo is on every card, with one exception: the reveal card.** There the photo is hidden behind a "?" only until revealed, and is always shown by the end of the card: a tap shows it, as a free hint that nothing records, and the right name flips the card if it hasn't been. A person gets one only after answering about them on the first try that day, with no hint and no reveal. Never for a card asked again after a hint, a photo-only card or someone without a photo, and the caregiver can turn reveal cards off for everyone or for one person.
- **The reveal unlock is never seen.** It lives in memory in `src/lib/reveal.ts`, for the day's deck only: never saved, never sent, never shown to the caregiver or the person, and never a score. Only `src/components/practice-screen.tsx` may import that file.
- **The 100% gate.** A person joins the practice deck only when their profile is 100% complete: every required question is answered or marked "Not applicable". `required` in `src/data/questionnaire.ts` defines the list; keep it short (name, photo, relationship, one interest, one shared memory). Below 100% a person stays a draft: saved, and shown in the People tab as "Draft, X% complete" with "Resume profile". Nobody is grandfathered, and someone who drops below 100% goes back to being a draft. **The percent** (`profileCompletion`, weights in `COMPLETION`): each required question done is an equal share (20); each optional question answered adds 3, at most 15, only while something required is missing; so it is 100 exactly when every required question is done, and optional answers never reach it. Only Review's Continue waits for 100%; every other step's Next is never gated.
- **The deck includes everyone unless deselected or archived.** Every complete person is in the deck, with nothing to tap. The caregiver's "Include in practice" toggle (the person's sheet, on by default) is the way out short of archiving: deselected people show "Not in practice" on the People tab, keep their profile, and are skipped by practice, hints, the names offered and the dashboard's counts. `inDeck` follows this rule on every change (`deckChanges` in `src/lib/profile.ts`).
- **Drafts never appear in the deck.** No practice cards, hints, offered names or person's screens come from a draft, a deselected person or an archived one. All of those read `deckOf` in `src/lib/profile.ts`, and RLS enforces it for the person too (`in_deck`, `include_in_practice`, `archived_at`). A story approved for a draft still waits for the profile.
- **Archive and delete.** Archiving is the caregiver's way to set someone aside, and it is fully reversible; deleting is the only way to lose anything.
  - **Archived** (`archivedAt` on the phone, `archived_at` on the server, missing or null while active) keeps everything: facts, photos, hint settings, approved stories and AI sentences, the schedule and practice history. It is stronger than "Include in practice" and separate from it: restoring brings the person back exactly as they were, with the include setting they had, and a draft comes back a draft.
  - **What it excludes:** everything practice shows, because `deckOf` leaves them out: every kind of card, reveal cards, hints, the names offered, the daily target and "X of N cards today", Practice more, AI generation (their sheet has no Generate), the approval queue, and every current-deck figure on Today and Trends. Past days and past totals don't change, and their history comes back with them. RLS keeps them, their cards and their photos from the person's login.
  - **The distractor rule:** an archived person's name is never offered as a choice. The names offered come only from the deck, so this holds on every kind of card, and old rotation memory can't bring a name back.
  - **Mid-day:** someone who leaves the deck during a day (archived, deselected, back to a draft) keeps the cards they finished in the day; their remaining cards go and nobody makes them up, and the day's target can't grow again that day, so the progress bar never moves back or past the end (`goalToday` in `src/lib/session.ts`). Restoring someone grows the day by their share, as adding someone does.
  - **Coming back:** restored after a day or more, their overdue cards return `RETURN_DUE_PER_DAY` a day (`src/config/schedule.ts`, a placeholder like the other pacing numbers), longest overdue first, each keeping its step (`spreadOnReturn` in `src/lib/queue.ts`). Restored the same day, as with Undo, nothing moves.
  - **Delete permanently** exists only on an archived person's sheet, behind a confirmation in the sheet that names them, says their photo, facts, stories and practice history go from the phone and the server and it can't be undone, and works only once "delete" is typed (`TypeToConfirm`). It removes every stored photo of theirs first (a failed photo delete stops it), then their row, facts, schedule and per-card practice rows, and on the phone what was kept of their practice, their card records for insights included. Day totals stay, since they name nobody. There are no test-data people, only test facts, so clearing test data clears archived people's test facts too and leaves them archived.
  - **Caregiver only.** The long press, the minus badges, Archive on the person's sheet and the Archived people section exist only on the caregiver's People tab. No person-facing screen gets them: the person's Practice more uses the same grid without them.
- **"I'm not sure" is asking for help, never a mistake** (since 2026-11-13). Every bound holds:
  - **The control.** A quiet `BigButton` (`testID="not-sure"`) under the names on every card with the hint ladder (blank, ask, who, photo-only, reverse and reveal cards), labelled **I’m not sure** and, for the tap that will show the answer, **Show me**; VoiceOver hears "I’m not sure. Show me a hint." (`NOT_SURE` in `src/lib/stories.ts`). It never shows once the answer is chosen or the hint has said it, and never on the success banner. No sound, haptics, shake or red; Reduce Motion fades the hint instead of sliding it; focus moves to the new hint.
  - **The same ladder.** Each tap is one hint step, as each other name chosen is (`src/lib/card-steps.ts`, pure): the existing hint words only, with "That is okay. Here is a little help." in place of "Take your time." on a first hint it brought. Fewer names sets aside the last other name chosen, or the first other name offered; never the answer. The person still ends the card by tapping the right name. Help taps and wrong taps share one ladder state: the card's handlers step on from the newest steps (`latest` in `PracticeCard`), so no tap is lost or counted twice.
  - **Recorded as help, not an answer.** A tap adds no selection. The card record keeps each tap and the step it reached (`help` in `src/lib/attempts.ts`; `help_at`, `help_steps` in `attempt_records`, steps from `HINT_STEPS`). Scheduling treats it like any hinted card (`cardResult`: tries are names chosen, every step a hint used), so it moves back a step, comes back in the session and gives no reveal unlock.
  - **Insights.** A card with a help request is "With help" (`resolvedOn`), with that group's points and no penalty of its own. Insights, the day sheet, the report and the data file show a plain count or list labelled "Asked for help", never as a failure, and only to the caregiver.
- **Practice is never blocked.** Once the day's session is complete, the person can keep practicing as often as they like, at any hour: the home shows **Keep practicing** (its one main button) and the calm, optional line "Nice work. You can keep practicing, or come back in the morning." No screen may say they can't practice, show when practice "opens", count down, or refuse a round. Extra practice records cards, gives hints and feedback like any card, and counts in Insights, labelled "Extra practice"; it never changes whether the day was finished.
- **The study day** (since 2026-11-12). A study day runs from 05:30 to 05:29:59 the next morning in the device's local time, keyed by the date it began on (02:00 on Nov 13 is study day Nov 12). The start is `DAY_START_HOUR`/`DAY_START_MINUTE` in `src/config/schedule.ts`, and `studyDayKey` in `src/lib/study-day.ts` is the only place the boundary is worked out: no other code computes "today" (`calendarKey` in `src/lib/activity.ts` is for calendar cells, labels and storage only; `scripts/check-logic.mjs` checks this). It decides only which day a card or session counts toward and when a new daily session begins:
  - the first full round of a study day completes it; an unfinished day stays recorded as not completed, and the next day starts fresh at 05:30, never carrying over
  - a round keeps the study day it began in to the end, even past 05:30, and is never cut off; practice at 23:00 or 03:00 never counts toward the next day
  - the spaced-retrieval day steps fall due at the start of a study day, counted in study days, not hours
  - completion, Today, Trends, Insights (calendar, Memory score, charts) group by study day; the score's calculation is unchanged; extra practice is left out of practice time against the standard only
  - each session and card record stores its `studyDay`, worked out on the device when its round began (`sessions.study_day`, `attempt_records.study_day` and `extra_practice` on the server); older rows without one fall back to `studyDayKey` of their time
  - the device's study-day clock (`src/data/study-clock.ts`, `evermore.studyDay.v1`) only moves forward, so a clock set back or travel west never hands out a day twice; it re-checks on returning to the front and on a timer to the next 05:30, and both practice stores move into the new day without a reload
  - each phone works out its own study day; a person's login reads (never writes) the family's sessions, so a day finished on one phone shows on the other by `study_day`
- **No developer tools in the app, and destructive actions are deliberate** (since 2026-11-12, `docs/SETTINGS-AUDIT.md`). No in-app control, in any build, may load sample or demo data, reset, clear or wipe data, force a score, a study day or a time, toggle a feature flag or the local-only mode, show test IDs, fake AI or simulate offline; no `__DEV__` section on any screen. A developer tool is a script, a test helper or a launch flag that defaults off and is stripped from release builds (`EXPO_PUBLIC_CLEAR_TEST_DATA=1`). `scripts/check-logic.mjs` fails if a screen refers to a sample loader, a clearing action, `__DEV__` or debug. Anything that deletes or overwrites data names exactly what is lost and where (this phone, the server, every phone), needs a deliberate second step (permanent deletion: "delete" typed, `TypeToConfirm`, never just OK), sits last and apart from frequent taps, and is undoable where it can be (archive first; Delete permanently only from Archived people). No action on the phone alone deletes server data, and no server action silently deletes what is only on the phone. Signing out never deletes caregiver data: it forgets this phone's copies only. Settings offers **Export my data** (the next rule).
- **Export my data** (since 2026-11-13). The caregiver's own family, complete, as a safety net after an accidental loss. Settings → Privacy and data, and **Export my data first** inside Delete account (the same panel swapped into that sheet). Every bound holds:
  - **Three files, each on request.** **Data file (everything)**: JSON (`familyExport`, `exportText` in `src/lib/export.ts`, format `EXPORT_FORMAT` 2), opening with the format, export date, app version, where it came from, whether it is complete, and a plain description of each section; then the settings, every person (drafts and archived people too, marked) with every profile answer as a fact with its question, the story practice says, its approval (approved, waiting, skipped) and dates, AI sentences with the facts they came from (sign-in off), every session, every card record kept (study day, extra practice, each answer chosen, the hints shown), the schedule, and linked phones' names and dates. **Report (PDF)**: `src/lib/insight-report.ts`, printed with `expo-print`. **Photos (zip)**: only once **Include photos** is ticked (off by default), the photos the caregiver added as stored, with `photos.json` saying whose each is, zipped with `fflate`; never the example deck's photos, an illustration, or a path outside the family's folder.
  - **Never in any of them:** logins, user ids of logins, User IDs, emails, passwords, tokens, secrets, device credentials, photo paths or links, test data, the demo, or a test fact's records. `scripts/check-export.mjs` checks every key and value.
  - **The caregiver's alone.** Only a signed-in caregiver's login reads anything (`caregiverId()` in `src/data/export-store.ts`, before any request); the person's login is refused, and RLS gives it no card record anyway. Every read uses the caregiver's own session, so RLS decides, and also names the family. Only `src/components/export-data.tsx` (shown only by Settings) imports `src/data/export-data.ts`, the one chooser; `scripts/check-logic.mjs` checks no person-facing screen reaches any export module, and `supabase/tests/rls.test.mjs` runs the export's own queries to show one family never reads another's.
  - **Full history, never a partial file silently.** Every table is read a page at a time (`EXPORT_PAGE`, 1000), with progress and Cancel. Any page that fails fails the whole read, with calm words for its kind (`EXPORT_PROBLEM`) and Try again. Offline, the caregiver may tap **Use this phone's copy** (`phoneCopySource`): the family as loaded this session, Insights' copy of the records and those still queued, 30 days of sessions; the file says it isn't complete and what it may lack, and so does the report. With sign-in off this phone is the whole copy (sessions are kept about a month there). Card records are kept 120 days everywhere, so that is the most the records and the report can cover; sessions go back to the start.
  - **The report** describes practice activity only, the Insights exception: its date range, study days practiced, sessions and extra practice, the Memory score by study day with the band names, how answers were found, each person (archived ones labelled) with "Found most readily" and "Needed the most support" within the family, a week-by-week table (Monday to Sunday by study day), and the notes: personal information at the top, "Practice activity, not a medical measure.", the 05:30 study day, and that the pacing numbers are placeholders still to be reviewed by a speech-language pathologist. Every figure comes from `src/lib/insights.ts` over the same records, so it matches Insights (archived people included, as with "Include archived"). The compact type scale and the Memory score's pinks; it is an insights file, so the banned words and claims checks read it.
  - **Files** are written to the app's temporary folder (`cache/exports`, `src/lib/save-file.ts`), handed to the share sheet, and deleted when it closes; leftovers go at the next export and at sign-out. Logs carry only the kind of a failure. There is no import or restore in the app: restoring is done by hand by the developer from the data file and the photos zip.
- **Real data stays off git** (since 2026-11-13). No real family data (names, photos, facts, relationships, emails, phone numbers) in any tracked file: code, tests, fixtures, docs, config or commit messages. Every bound holds:
  - **The committed deck is fictional.** `src/data/sample-deck.ts` holds the five fictional sample people (June, Leo, Ruth, Omar, Mae), with illustrations (`src/data/portraits.ts`), the same five the server seeds. Tests and fixtures use invented people (check-logic's stand-ins for real people are invented too). Never add a real person to code.
  - **Your real cards live only in gitignored files on your computer:** `src/data/deck.local.ts` (`export const localDeck: Person[]`, the same shape as the sample deck) and photos in `assets/photos/` named by id. `src/data/example-deck.ts` reads the local file only when `__DEV__` and only if it exists (`require.context`); missing or malformed, the app uses the sample deck with no error. Nothing else may read it, and only `src/data/local-store.ts` reads the example deck. Its people are held back from AI (`HELD_BACK_FROM_AI`).
  - **The guard.** `scripts/check-no-real-data.mjs` (in `npm test`) fails if any entry of your private denylist, `scripts/private-denylist.local.txt` (gitignored; you fill it in; the format is in `scripts/private-denylist.example.txt`), appears in a tracked or new file, and if git tracks a photo under `assets/photos/` (only its README), the local deck or the denylist. Without a denylist it warns and passes. It never prints an entry: only its line number and where. `scripts/check-no-real-data.test.mjs` proves it fails on a planted word and passes once it's gone. Entries: `Name` (whole word, any capitalization, so ids such as `name-0` too), `=Word` (exact capitalization, for a name that is also an ordinary word), `~Word` (also a word libraries use: files only, not bundles).
  - **Adding real people safely:** through the app on a device (stored on the phone, or in the caregiver's own account on the server), never in code. For personal testing in development builds only, add them to `src/data/deck.local.ts`, and add their names to the denylist.
- **The real family stays out of release builds.** `src/data/photos.ts` and `src/data/example-deck.ts` load the real photos and `src/data/deck.local.ts` only when `__DEV__`; nothing that ships imports the local file; `.easignore` keeps the photos, the local deck, the denylist and `.env` out of any EAS upload; and `scripts/check-release.mjs` builds the iOS and Android release bundles and fails on any real photo, any local card's words, any denylist entry, a secret or non-public `.env` value, or a developer control, and if git tracks the local deck, the denylist or a photo. Every failure log goes through `warnKind` (`src/lib/log.ts`): what failed and its kind only, never a row, name or message.
- **The daily reminder** (since 2026-11-13). One gentle, local notification on the person's own phone, configured there only: no caregiver control, no server, no push service, nothing synced, nothing in an export. Every bound holds:
  - **Off until the person says yes.** After the person's first completed session, the home asks once, in a sheet, "Would you like a gentle reminder on this phone each day to practice?" with **Yes, remind me** and **Not now** (`ReminderQuestion` in `src/components/reminders.tsx`), never over another screen. Not now, or a refusal at the system prompt, is final: it is never asked again automatically (only whether it was asked survives a sign-out), and the app works the same. The person changes it in their own settings: **Reminders** under the sizes in the Aa sheet (on or off, and the time with Earlier and Later, 30 minutes a step, 06:00 to 21:00). With the system permission off, a calm line says how to turn it on and **Open settings** opens the app's page in the phone's Settings. The caregiver's Text size sheet has no Reminders row, and no caregiver screen mentions them.
  - **One fixed message:** "Your practice is ready whenever you are." (`REMINDER_TEXT` in `src/lib/reminders.ts`), the body only: never a name, fact, photo, score, count, streak, "missed", urgency or emoji, and no title or data. Shown by the system, never in the app's foreground.
  - **At most one a study day, no follow-ups.** The default is 09:00 local (`REMINDER_HOUR`, `REMINDER_MINUTE` in `src/config/schedule.ts`, placeholders a clinician should confirm), always after the 05:30 start, so a reminder belongs to its date's study day. Each study day has one identifier (`evermore.reminder.<study day>`), and a day whose reminder fired (no longer waiting in the system) or whose time passed counts as reminded, so a changed time, the clock moving or a new time zone can't remind twice.
  - **Rebuilt, never stale.** A notification can't look at the app when it fires, so the phone schedules only the next `REMINDER_DAYS_AHEAD` (3) days' reminders, none today once the day's session is complete and none at all with nothing to practice, and rebuilds them (`rebuildReminders`) when the home opens, comes to the front, the session is completed, a new study day begins at 05:30, and any setting changes. "Today" is `studyToday()` from the study clock, never anything else.
  - **Signing out cancels them**, every way it happens (`forgetReminders` in `src/data/forget.ts`), including a phone the caregiver removed, as soon as it checks in; and a launch signed out or as the caregiver keeps none (`src/app/_layout.tsx`). A phone removed while closed can remind at most `REMINDER_DAYS_AHEAD` more times before it next opens.
  - **It changes nothing.** It never blocks or changes practice, and records nothing anywhere. Its settings are `evermore.reminders.v1` in AsyncStorage on that phone. Failures (permission refused, a system call failing) are silent, logged through `warnKind` only.
- **Two people to practice.** Practice needs at least two people in the deck (`MIN_PEOPLE` in `src/lib/session.ts`), so every card offers a real choice. With fewer there is no target and no session of any kind; the person's home shows its calm line and Today says "Add or restore people to start practicing." A day already begun keeps its progress on Today. With two people every card offers two names, and the fewer-names hint never leaves just one.
- **"Not applicable".** Offered only on required questions where an empty answer is legitimate (`mayNotApply` on the question: photo, relationship, one interest, one shared memory), so the gate stays honest: nobody should invent a fact to reach 100%. Never on the name, and never on an optional question, which can simply stay empty. It counts toward completion, creates no fact, and is shown on the review step. An answer replaces it. Required questions carry a small asterisk, and each step with a required question has a "* Required" legend (no other step does). Optional questions have no marker. Never use the phrase "Do not fill out".
- **No invented facts.** Everything practice says comes from what a caregiver entered. test_data is the only exception for real people, for testing only: flagged on every value (`testData`, `testMarks`), local to the phone (never sent to the server, never seeding it, never in invites or AI input), tagged "Test data" on the caregiver's screens, and removable with a development-only launch flag (`EXPO_PUBLIC_CLEAR_TEST_DATA=1`), never a control in the app. Real people's sentences stay exactly as written, and their test values are generic and harmless, never anything that could be taken for a true statement about their life. Your real people's cards (the local deck) always keep this flag. The 5 fictional sample people describe nobody, so their invented profile facts are ordinary facts, not test_data: unflagged, kept when test data is cleared, and usable for testing AI end to end. AI input goes through `withoutTestData`, in `sendableFacts` (`src/lib/ai-request.ts`). The local deck's real people are also held back from AI by person (`HELD_BACK_FROM_AI`), because their deck facts carry no flag of their own.
- **Consent and the privacy policy** (since 2026-11-13). **No data is collected before agreement.** Every bound holds:

Without both, `signUpCaregiver` sends nothing, and the sign-up trigger in `supabase/schema.sql` refuses the account anyway.
  - **The research choice is optional and off**, a third unticked box at sign-up and **Research use** in Settings, "You can change this at any time", with no effect on the app either way. Nothing is used for research, and no research export or report exists: building one needs a policy change first.
  - **Versioned records** in `public.consents`: kind (`policy`, `responsibility`, `research`), `policy_version`, the choice, the server's time. Added, never changed or removed, except with the whole account. The caregiver reads and adds only their own; the person's login reads none. Research changes are each a new record, the newest in force. Caregivers from before versions have one `legacy` record.
  - **A new version asks again.** `POLICY_VERSION` in `src/lib/consent.ts` is the date of the words in force. When a caregiver's records lack both agreements for it, `PolicyGate` (`src/components/consent.tsx`, in the caregiver layout) covers every caregiver screen with no Close: Agree and continue, or Sign out. Nothing is lost while it waits. It asks only on a fresh answer from the server (`src/data/consent-store.ts`, nothing kept on the phone), so an offline caregiver is never locked out. Cards that come back after a hint move the schedule but don't count toward the day, so the total can't reveal how answers went. `attempts.tries`, `first_try_correct` and `hints_used` drive scheduling only and are unreadable through the API by design. Keep them that way. Missed days have no score and nothing counts against them: no streaks, no zeros.

A record holds ids, fixed words, numbers and times, never a name, a photo or anyone's words (`src/lib/attempt-records.ts` is that line), and test data and the demo never leave the phone. `attempts`' correctness columns stay write-only for everyone. Test data (and the demo) counts only in demo mode; archived people only with "Include archived". Days from before records were kept show nothing, never an invented value.

Never use the words diagnosis, decline, progression, severity or accuracy in the UI, and never "missed" or "failed" for a day. Insights also never say wrong, failed, failure or disease, and never call a single card a score.

## Project

Evermore as an Expo SDK 58 app (Expo Router, TypeScript, React Compiler), run in Expo Go on a physical phone, so only modules bundled in Expo Go can be used. Supabase is the backend: the URL and publishable key come from `.env` (`EXPO_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_URL`, `EXPO_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_ANON_KEY`). `README.md` has the Supabase setup. Sign-in, and with it every server call but one, is behind `AUTH_ENABLED` in `src/config/features.ts`, which is on by default; starting with `EXPO_PUBLIC_LOCAL_ONLY=1` (`npm run start:local`) turns it off for development (see Architecture). The one exception is the AI call, behind its own flag. An earlier web version (Vite PWA) lives in `~/evermore` and is no longer developed.

## Architecture

**Sign-in switch.** `AUTH_ENABLED` in `src/config/features.ts`: on by default, off when the app is started with `EXPO_PUBLIC_LOCAL_ONLY=1` (`npm run start:local`). Release bundles inline the value; development reads it through Expo's env module, which Metro fills from the shell and `.env`.
- Off (local-only): no Supabase client is created (`src/lib/supabase.ts` hands out a stand-in that throws), so nothing can reach the server, except the AI call (`src/data/ai-client.ts`), which needs no client. `src/data/local-store.ts` keeps everything in AsyncStorage:
  - the family (people, facts, approvals, hint settings, phrasings turned off, AI sentences, and when someone was archived) under `evermore.family.v1`, copied once from the example deck (`src/data/example-deck.ts`: the fictional `src/data/sample-deck.ts`, and in development the gitignored `src/data/deck.local.ts` if present; photos via `src/data/photos.ts`) with the card ids practice already used (`<person id>-<index>`); a deleted deck person isn't copied back, and the deck files never change
  - test data, copied in once by `seedTestData` (`src/lib/local-family.ts`) from `src/data/test-data.ts`, which adds the three sample people the deck lacks and fills each deck person's required gaps, flagged for real people and as ordinary `sample-` facts for sample people; `clearTestData` removes the flagged ones for good, and `releaseSampleFacts` converts sample facts saved as test data by an earlier release
  - the schedule, sessions, finished cards, the last phrasing per person, the session length and reveal setting, the AI switch and consent, whether demo insights are on, and today's log (what was asked, and the deck and target `goalToday` last worked out) under `evermore.local.v1`
  - each card's record for insights under `evermore.attempts.v1.<YYYY-MM>` (see Insights below)
  - Pure rules: `src/lib/local-family.ts` and `src/lib/local-practice.ts`. Photos picked here are copied into the app's own folder (`keepPhoto` in `src/lib/photo-files.ts`).
  - The person is the role until Caregiver is held; `openCaregiver`/`closeCaregiver` switch it, and `practiceWith` hands the phone back straight into one person's cards.
- On: everything below.
- `src/data/app-data.ts` is the only place that chooses: `useWho`, the practice actions, `useDashboard`, `setSessionLength`, `setRevealCards`, the family (`useFamilyView` and its actions: `addPerson`, `updatePerson`, `saveFacts`, `setCardStatus`, `editCard`, `setHints`, `setPhrasingsOff`, `setProfile`, `addToPractice`, `setIncludeInPractice`, `setRevealOk`, `setFactHints`, `approveAll`, `undoApproveAll`, `archivePerson`, `restorePerson`, `deletePerson`, `clearTestData`, and the AI actions: `generateQuestions`, `agreeToAi`, `setAiOn`, `setAiOk`, `approveAiSentence`, `editAiSentence`, `rejectAiSentence`, `approveAllSentences`) and the hand-over. Screens that work both ways import from it. The server and phone stores share shapes (`src/data/family-store.ts`, `src/lib/facts.ts`) and never import each other.

**Accounts.** `src/lib/supabase.ts` creates the one client, with its session in `src/lib/session-storage.ts`: `expo-secure-store` in pieces under 2048 bytes (`AFTER_FIRST_UNLOCK_THIS_DEVICE_ONLY`), moving a session an earlier release kept in AsyncStorage, and telling a store it can't read apart from an empty one (`.web.ts`: localStorage, for the test build). There is no "Keep me signed in": a phone stays signed in until signed out. `src/data/account.ts` is a `useSyncExternalStore` store (`useAccount()`, and `accountNow`/`watchAccount` outside React) fed by `onAuthStateChange`. It opens at once from the kept session and the saved profile (`evermore.profile.<id>`, with the email), because with an expired token and no connection supabase-js retries for about 25 seconds and then reports no session while keeping it; a session supabase-js removes signs out. It holds every account action: sign-in with the lockout, caregiver sign-up with both agreements (`signUpData`; `checkEmail` comes from Supabase's answer), the confirmation email (resend, code), password reset, Change password (current password first), sign-out (offline too: it forgets the session itself, and an older build's credential with it), and calls to the `manage-family` function. Every sign-out runs the cleanups `src/data/forget.ts` registers (imported by `app-data.ts`), for every login with a saved profile. Calm words for every answer, and the form rules, are in `src/lib/auth-form.ts`. Screen 1's steps are in `src/components/sign-in/` (welcome; for the person, "Hello" in `person-sign-in.tsx`, with "Your caregiver set this up for you." and the policy link; caregiver sign-in, sign-up with "Check your email", Forgot password with the code from the email), with `PasswordField` (Show/Hide, the strength hint) in `src/components/password-field.tsx`. The functions are called at the URL slugs in `src/config/functions.ts`, as deployed: `manage-family` at `dynamic-api` and `generate-questions` at `clever-service`. The folder names under `supabase/functions/` stay the functions' own names. A person's User ID maps to `<id>@person.evermore.invalid` (`personEmail`), kept in step with the function.

**Routing.** `src/app/_layout.tsx` waits for fonts, the saved text size and `useWho()`, then guards three groups with `Stack.Protected`:
- signed out (sign-in on only): `sign-in`, whose steps (welcome, the person's Hello, caregiver forms) are state inside the one route, with Android's back button going back a step
- person: `index` (home) and `practice`
- caregiver: the `caregiver` group, a nested stack (`src/app/caregiver/_layout.tsx`) whose first route is the dashboard. Without the group, Expo Router fell back to the first caregiver route alphabetically (`caregiver/add`) after sign-in.

**Caregiver data.** `src/data/family-store.ts` (`useFamily()`) loads the people (with hint settings), cards (one fact each), practice settings and the person's User ID login (the one person profile with a username; a login an earlier build made for a phone has none and is left alone) for the signed-in caregiver, and reloads when a different caregiver signs in. Its actions write to Supabase, then reload (after waiting for any load already running, which may hold old rows):
- `addPerson`, which uploads the photo to `photos/<caregiver_id>/<person id>.<ext>` with `cacheControl: '0'`. With the default (an hour), Supabase's CDN kept serving a deleted photo through any link that had already been opened.
- `updatePerson`: a replaced photo gets a new file name (the phone caches photos by path); a new relationship rewrites every sentence and sends approved facts back for approval
- `saveFacts` (part of a form: new, changed and emptied facts; facts it doesn't mention stay, so each step saves by itself; memory photos go to the same folder), `editCard`, `setCardStatus` (approving a fact that may change confirms it)
- `setHints`, `setPhrasingsOff`, `setProfile` (profile step, best clue, what is not applicable), `addToPractice` and `setIncludeInPractice` (the caregiver's say; `in_deck` follows on every load), `setRevealOk`, `setFactHints` (which facts may be hints; with none left, the fact hint turns off)
- `archivePerson` (shown at once: `archived_at` and `in_deck` false), `restorePerson` (moves their overdue schedule rows first, with `spreadOnReturn`, newer than the person's phone's copy), `deletePerson` (archived people only: lists the family's folder so leftovers from a failed photo replacement go too, removes every photo of theirs, and only then deletes the row; the foreign keys take facts, schedule and attempts)
- `setSessionLength`, `setRevealCards`

**The day's practice.** `src/lib/session.ts` (pure) turns the deck into a study session:
- `dailyTarget`: about 2, 3 or 5 questions per person (Session length, `SESSION_LENGTHS`), within limits that keep a Standard day to 8 to 12 minutes, capped by the different questions the deck has and by what can be kept apart, and none with fewer than `MIN_PEOPLE` (2). `goalToday` is what both phone stores show: the target, plus what someone who left the deck today finished, with a ceiling for the rest of that day once anyone leaves (kept in today's log with the deck it was worked out from), cleared when someone joins. A smaller deck can allow more, because fewer people need less spacing, so without the ceiling archiving could move the bar back. With sign-in on, the caregiver's dashboard can't see the person's log and shows `dailyTarget`. `goal` everywhere is this number, recorded on each session.
- `planToday`: what's left of the day, each card narrowed to the one phrasing it will use. Due first, then new; different facts and shapes for each person; no sentence twice in a day (`sentenceKey`: its words, or for "Who is this?" whose photo it shows); the same person at least 4 cards apart (`interleave`, which only takes a card if the rest of the day can still fit). What was asked is kept on the phone (`today`, `logAsked`), so all of this holds across a stop and a resume.
- `reaskSpot`, `reviewPass`, `askedAgain`: a card that needed a hint comes back at expanding gaps (`SAME_SESSION_MINUTES`, about 1, 2, 4 and 8 minutes as cards, at most `SAME_SESSION_MOST` a session; another hint starts the series again), and a short review pass at the end. These cards have `again` set, ask with the richest cue left, and don't count toward the day.
- Cue fading: `nearestCue` narrows each choice of phrasing to the cue levels `CUE_FADING` (`src/config/schedule.ts`) prefers for the card's schedule step (`cueLevel` in `src/lib/stories.ts`): the relationship for a new card, the face alone or the reverse card only as it climbs. A preference within the day's other rules, never a lock.
- `todayYouSaw`: the wrap-up's faces and names (at most `TODAY_YOU_SAW`), everyone with a photo whose card came up, in first-seen order, so it says nothing about the answers.
- `planMore`, `planPerson`: Practice more and one person's cards.
- `estimatedMinutes` and `todayLine`: cards times `SECONDS_PER_CARD`, for "About 8 minutes today". `SECONDS_PER_CARD` is the sum of `CARD_SECONDS`, a documented average, not something the app enforces. If a real Standard day runs under about 8 minutes, raise `perPerson` for Standard, and Long in proportion.
- `nextTapCounts`: whether a tap on Next counts. It reads only the clock, so no kind of card, hint or flip can hold Next back.
- `cardsOf` and `practiceFrom`: the deck as practice reads it, used by both stores, so the caregiver's dashboard and the person's phone get the same target.

**Facts to cards.** A card is one fact: a kind (`FACT_TYPES`), the caregiver's words, whether it may change (then `asOf`, and `confirmedAt` once confirmed), a memory's `year` and photo, its `source`, `phrasingsOff` and `hintOk`. `src/lib/facts.ts` holds the rules both stores follow (`inPractice`, `statusChange`, `planFacts`). A new kind of fact needs its place in `FACT_TYPES`, `PREDICATES` and `PHRASINGS`, a question, and `cards_fact_type_check` in the schema. `src/lib/stories.ts` holds every word practice can show:
- `sentenceFor`: the approved sentence, `My {relationship}, {name}, {predicate}.` or `{name} {predicate}.`, the predicate being the words in their kind's fixed phrase. It is saved to `cards.sentence`.
- `phrasingsFor`: 3 to 6 fixed shapes per kind, of four kinds (blank, ask, who, photo); a fact that may change leads with "As of {month}" in each.
- `makeRounds`/`roundFor`: queue order, a phrasing other than the one this person saw last, and the answer plus two other names from `chooseNames`, which rotates the other names and the right name's place (`lastPhrasing` and `lastChoices`, kept on the phone).
- `hintAt`: the ladder in `HINT_LADDER` order (another fact, the relationship, the letter, fewer names) as the person's hint settings allow, then the answer itself (`show`: "It’s June." and that name's button selected). The relationship rung is skipped when the card already says it, and on reverse cards. `HINT_RUNGS` is the stored set (the schema's order); `HINT_LADDER` is the order. The fact rung follows the best clue and skips facts with `hintOk` false.
- `chooseNames` never offers names that sound alike (`soundsAlike`: the same first letter, or a spelling a letter or two apart) while the deck has others, nor any name twice. `phrasingsFor` leaves out a question that would say the person's name, and `cardsOf` drops a card with nothing left to ask.

## Privacy

- `assets/photos/` and `src/data/deck.local.ts` hold the user's real family cards. Both are gitignored and in `.easignore`, and stay on the user's computer and phone only:
  - Never upload, migrate, seed, delete or modify them.
  - Only sign-in-off mode shows them, but Metro bundles them whichever way `AUTH_ENABLED` is set, so they travel from the dev server to the phone. Prefer the same Wi-Fi to `--tunnel`, which relays the bundle through a tunnel service.
  - Apart from its README, `assets/photos/` is in `.gitignore`. Never commit or publish the photos, the local deck or the denylist, and never copy a real name or fact into a tracked file, a commit message or a reply.
  - The git history before 2026-11-13 (on GitHub too) still holds the real cards: `HANDOFF.md` has the findings and the steps to remove them.
- Never run `eas update` or `eas build`: both upload the bundle to Expo's servers.
- The server seed (`seed_sample_people` in `supabase/schema.sql`) has only fictional people with illustrated portraits.
- AI sends what the first hard rule and the privacy line allow, and no more. A name that can't be told from an ordinary word isn't masked: one typed in lowercase, or one that is also a month, unless it is a family member's. Writing with no capitals can't be read for names, so a fact in it isn't sent at all.
- **Agreements:** the caregiver's consent records (`public.consents`) hold a kind, a policy version, a choice and a time: no words, email or device. They are read fresh from the server and never kept on the phone, and go with the account.
- **Reminders:** nothing leaves the phone. The reminder is scheduled and shown by the phone itself, says only the one fixed sentence, and its settings stay on that phone.
- Never identify or recognize anyone from a photo. Names and relationships come only from what the caregiver typed.
- After checking something, delete exports and screenshots that contain real photos.
- **Exports:** they contain personal information (names, stories, how practice went, and photos when asked for) and are created only on the caregiver's request, on the caregiver's phone, from the caregiver's own login. The app never sends them anywhere: the share sheet is the caregiver's choice. Restoring from an export is a manual process by the developer, not in the app.
- **Phones linked by an earlier build:** their names and dates stay in `devices` on the server, shown nowhere and never exported, until the caregiver deletes the account or the developer drops the tables by hand.
- The privacy policy (`src/lib/privacy-policy.ts`, shown by `src/components/privacy-policy.tsx`) is a draft that needs legal review, and `docs/DATA-INVENTORY.md` is what it rests on. Keep both in step with what is stored, and rerun `node scripts/build-policy.mjs`. Its "AI questions" section and the consent on the person's sheet (`AiConsent`) say the same thing. `docs/app-store-privacy-answers.md` and `docs/testflight-checklist.md` hold the drafts for App Store Connect.

- **Sign-in on in the web build, without the live project** (the default build now). Run the stand-in, `node scripts/fakes/supabase-server.mjs 54321`, and export a throwaway copy with `.env` pointing at it (`EXPO_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_URL=http://localhost:54321`, any key) and blank stand-ins named like the files in `assets/photos/`, so the real photos are never in the export. Its `/__standin/` controls (POST) set Auth's settings (`settings`: `autoconfirm` is "Confirm email" off, `offline`, `emailSeconds`, `signInLimit`), list the emails sent with their codes (`emails`), open a confirmation link (`click-link`), make a caregiver's sample people ready to practice (`ready-deck`) and expire tokens (`expire`). The session is in `localStorage` under `sb-localhost-auth-token`. A shown password's input has no `type` attribute on web (not `text`). React Native Web has no pull to refresh: opening Insights fetches what is new.
- **The local-only build** is an export with `EXPO_PUBLIC_LOCAL_ONLY=1` set in the shell (and `--clear`).
- **Check the audit itself** before trusting a walk: plant a red word, an off-palette color, a target under the minimum and an element wider than the page, and each must fail it. Compare colors by property, not value: a border's color defaults to the text's, so a value comparison silently skips every text color.
- **Exports from more than one copy of the project share Metro's cache,** and expo-router's list of routes is cached with the first copy's folder, so a later export can mix old screens with new code. Export each copy with `--clear`, and start the real project with `--clear` afterwards.
- **Archiving in the web build.** A long press is the mouse held down past 400 ms. React Native Web always reports a screen reader, so the Undo toast stays its longer 15 seconds there. Delete's confirmation is `window.confirm`: answer it from Playwright's `dialog` event. Seed `evermore.local.v1` with a session and a `today` log to check the day's target when someone is archived mid-day.
- **Reminders.** Local notifications work in Expo Go on iOS and Android (only push needs a development build since SDK 53; `expo-notifications` logs a warning in Expo Go saying so). The web build has none: `notifier.web.ts` stands in, and a walk can seed `evermore.reminderStandIn.v1` with `permission: 'denied'` to see the calm line, and read what is waiting from it. To check the question, seed `evermore.local.v1` with a finished session for today's study day. Only a phone shows a reminder actually firing, the system prompt, Open settings, a restart, a time zone change while closed, and daylight saving; a development build or TestFlight is needed for the Android notification icon and the build's entitlements.
- **Signed photo links and the CDN.** Supabase's CDN caches signed photo URLs by path and token, ignoring other query parameters. To check that a photo is gone, sign a fresh URL a few seconds later instead of refetching an old one.
