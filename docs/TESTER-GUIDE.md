# Welcome to the Tandem test

Thank you for helping test Tandem. This page explains what it is, how to install it, and how to tell us what you think. It takes about five minutes to read.

## What Tandem is

Tandem is an iPhone app for families. A caregiver adds the important people in someone's life, each with a photo and a few short facts. Tandem turns those facts into little fill-in-the-blank stories, like "My aunt, ______, grows tomatoes and shares them every summer." The person being helped, someone with early memory loss, practices a few of these cards a day on their own phone by tapping the right name.

There are two sides, each with its own login:

- **The caregiver**, who adds people, approves every story, and sees how practice is going.
- **The person practicing**, who signs in with a User ID and password the caregiver makes for them.

Tandem is a practice tool, not a medical device. It does not test for, treat or measure any health condition. "Tandem" is a working name and may change.

## Only made-up people

Every new account starts with five made-up sample people, June, Leo, Ruth, Omar and Mae, with drawn portraits. They describe nobody real.

No real family's cards or photos are in this test version. The ones the developer uses while building the app stay on the developer's own computer and are never put in a TestFlight build.

You're welcome to add people you know. Because this is a test and the privacy policy is still a draft, you may prefer to use the sample people, or to add only people who have said it's okay.

## Installing it

1. On your iPhone, install **TestFlight** from the App Store. It is Apple's free app for testing apps before they are released.
2. Open the invitation email (or the link we sent you) on the same iPhone, and tap **View in TestFlight**, or **Accept**.
3. In TestFlight, tap **Install**. Tandem then appears on your home screen like any other app.

Each test version lasts 90 days. When there's a new one, TestFlight offers an **Update**.

## Trying it out

1. Open Tandem, tap **I am a caregiver**, then **Create an account**. Use an email address you can read: we send a 6-digit code to confirm it.
2. Open a sample person and tap **Resume profile** to finish them, or add someone under **People**. Approve their stories. Practice starts once two people are finished.
3. Make a login for the person practicing: **Settings**, then **The person's phone**, then **Their login**.
4. On a second iPhone (or after **Settings**, then **Sign out**), tap **I am practicing** and sign in with that User ID and password. Tap **Start** and practice a few cards.

If you forget your password, tap **Forgot password?** on the sign-in screen, and we email you a code.

## Sending feedback

Anything is useful: something confusing, too small to read, slow, or broken.

- **A screenshot, straight from the app.** Take a screenshot as usual (the side button and volume up together). Tap the small picture that appears, tap the share button, then **Share Beta Feedback**. Add a note and tap **Submit**.
- **A note without a screenshot.** Open **TestFlight**, tap **Tandem**, then **Send Beta Feedback**.
- **Email.** Write to tandemsupport26@gmail.com.

Screenshots go to the developer through Apple. Please leave out real people's photos or anything private you'd rather not share. The sample people are fine.

## Your data

- **Export it.** **Settings**, **Privacy and data**, **Export my data**. You get a file of everything you entered, a report, and your photos if you ask for them.
- **Delete it.** **Settings**, **Delete account** (last on the page). Type "delete" to confirm. That deletes your account, every person, photo, story and practice record, and the person's login, from our servers straight away. It can't be undone.
- Deleting the app from your phone does **not** delete your account. Use **Delete account** first.

## Please keep in mind

- This is a test version. Things may change, look different or break, and you may be asked to update.
- The privacy policy is a draft awaiting review by a lawyer or privacy professional. You can read it in the app: **Settings**, **Privacy and data**, **Privacy policy**.

Thank you again for your time and your honesty.
